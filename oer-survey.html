4. Does your course use low-cost material or no-cost material? *

Low cost is considered to be <$50. Zero cost can be OER or non-OER digital material. Other scenarios include if your department pays for guaranteed access to a text at no cost to students.

6. Is your textbook or material considered an Open Educational Resource (OER) or is it non-OER digital material? *

Non-OER digital material can include but are not restricted to Canvas instructional resources (custom-made lectures) & college-provided resources (such as library databases, eBooks through Libby, or library-provided access to The New York Times).

OER Material Information

What is the resource? This includes Pressbooks, LibreTexts, OpenStax, OTL, UML, and more. This does not include PDFs of textbooks from publishers, Google Books samples, self-hosted resources on a private Google Drive, etc.
